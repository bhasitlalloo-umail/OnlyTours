Featured Attractions
Cascade 500 pieds
Discover one's of Mauritius's best kept secrets. This dramatic 500 foot waterfall drops into the Black River Gorges, ending at a natural infinity pool with sweeping views over the south coast.
Difficulty : Hard
Le Morne Brabant
Le Morne is a spectacular peninsula and mountain in the southwest of Mauritius, located approximately 60 km from Port Louis. Dominated by Le Morne Brabant, which rises to 555 meters, this exceptional site is listed as a UNESCO World Heritage site as a place of memory for escaped slaves.
Trou aux Cerfs
Trou aux Cerfs is a famous tourist attraction known for its beautiful flora. It comprises of a dormant volcano crater.
